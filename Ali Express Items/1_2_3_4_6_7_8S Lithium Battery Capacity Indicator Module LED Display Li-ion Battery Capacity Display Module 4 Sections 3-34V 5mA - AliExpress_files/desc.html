Display battery quantity parameters: (Note: N represents the battery quantity)
1. When the battery voltage exceeds N*3.3V, 1 battery will be illuminated
2. When the battery voltage exceeds N*3.5V, 2 blocks of electricity will be illuminated
3. When the battery voltage exceeds N*3.7V, 3 blocks of electricity will be illuminated
4. When the battery voltage exceeds N*3.9V, 4 blocks of electricity will be illuminated
5. When the battery voltage is less than N*3.3V, the four displays will turn off

This power display panel can be used for both lithium lead-acid and nickel-metal hydride batteries, as long as the required voltage is within the range of the parameter table.
