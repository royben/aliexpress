How To Install:

1. Clean and Dry the desired location(flat position).

2. Remove the adhesive, stick on the desired flat location.

3. It's better to use a hair dryer to heat the glue surface to make it more sticky.

4. Press well the emblem(do not pull or move it again).

5. Leave it alone without washing within 72 hours.

How To Remove: Use a hair dryer to heat the sticker, then peel off the sticker easily from the car.
