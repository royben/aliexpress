Connection Steps for Two Modes


① 2.4G Wireless Connection
1. Insert the battery
2. Plug USB receiver into device
3. Switch the mode to 2.4G
4. Click and move the mouse
② Bluetooth 5.2 Connection
1. Insert the battery
2. Switch the mode to Bluetooth
3. Press the DPI button for 2 seconds
4. Turn on your device's Bluetooth
5. Connecting "BaseusF02Mouse"
6. Click and move the mouse

(Note: The battery is not included in the package)
