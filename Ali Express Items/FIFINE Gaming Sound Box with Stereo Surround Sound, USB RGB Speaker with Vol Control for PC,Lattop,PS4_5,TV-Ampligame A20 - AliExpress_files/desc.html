Outstanding Aural Effects

The FIFINE A20 speaker has shocking rich bass,large volume without voice break, 360° all around sound effect. It provides the perfect atmosphere for your entertainment activities such as watching movies, gaming, and listening to music. With its excellent highs and powerful lows for an immersive audio experience.


Uniquely Crafted Design

With its stereo sound output and a 6° angled design, the FIFINE A20 creates a captivating sound stage that surrounds the user's ears, resulting in unparalleled audio immersion and clarity.


Excellent Function Key Design

The main box of the FIFINE A20 is equipped with touch-sensitive RGB and volume keys. Adjusting the volume is a breeze - a short or long press will instantly adjust the sound output. For added convenience, a quick press of less than 2 seconds will mute the sound with just one touch. Meanwhile, the auxiliary box features a power key that can be easily controlled with a short press to mute and a long press to turn the power on/off.


RGB Colorful Light Show

Not only does the FIFINE A20 a speaker, but also as a unique gaming theme light, it provide different color-changing themes. Featuring a touch-sensitive RGB key, it allows you to effortlessly switch between 11 different colors, including single color, breathing gradient, and dynamic gradient modes. It's as simple as a touch, giving you full control over the mesmerizing light display.



Intelligent Memory Function

Additionally, the FIFINE A20 boasts an intelligent memory function. Once powered on, it automatically sets the volume to the same level as the previous use, ensuring a consistent audio experience. Moreover, the RGB lighting effects will also remember the last setting, so you can easily pick up where you left off with your preferred lighting experience.




Easy to Use with USB Connector

The FIFINE A20 is compatible with a wide range of devices, including desktop computers, laptops, TVs, monitors, and mobile devices that feature a 3.5mm audio jack. Note: You need to use 5V/1A plug to connect the power supply, otherwise it will damage the normal operation and life of the speaker.
