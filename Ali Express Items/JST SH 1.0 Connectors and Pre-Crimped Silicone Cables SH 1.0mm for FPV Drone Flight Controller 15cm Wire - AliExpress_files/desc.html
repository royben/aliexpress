Features:

This connector is compatible with JST SH 1.0mm connector. You can make an arbitrary cable by just plugging the terminal of the cable into the connector manually. So you can array the color order arbitrarily as you want. You can also replace or fix the broken cable in your hand. Ultra-soft silicone wire still has very good flexibility at low temperatures. so it can eliminate the vibration transferred to the flight controller.
