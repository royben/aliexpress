Description


Auto stop function:Equipped with an auto shut-off feature, the compressor activates when you press the airbrush trigger and stops when you release it, promoting efficiency and extending the lifespan of both the compressor and the airbrush.This airbrush compressor packs impressive power into its small frame, delivering approximately 30 PSI of air pressure. To safeguard your work surface and minimize vibrations, it features rubber feet. Weighing in at just around 5 pounds, this compressor is highly portable, running on 12V DC (an AC adapter is provided). It also includes a detachable 6-foot nylon air hose with a standard 1/8" BSP fitting.


Enhanced Functionality:The airbrush pen's trigger allows you to adjust both pigment flow and air supply. This airbrush compressor comes with a double-action airbrush that provides precise control over both fluid volume and air pressure. Additionally, the cleverly designed open nozzle cap shields the needle, ensuring it remains protected without any hindrance. The set includes three needle types: 0.2mm, 0.3mm, and 0.5mm, offering versatility for various applications. Crafted from high-quality aluminum, the airbrush is remarkably lightweight. Its trigger mechanism operates smoothly and responsively, while the ergonomic design of the airbrush's end ensures comfort during extended use.


Effortless Cleaning:The airbrush's cutaway handle design facilitates quick and easy flushing and cleaning of air passages. The fluid control knob allows for seamless adjustment, giving you precise control over paint flow.


All-Powerful Accessories:This airbrush kit comes with a wealth of accessories that fully meet your needs, eliminating the need to purchase additional items separately. It even includes a professional airbrush cleaning kit to ensure your airbrush stays clean at all times. Replacement o-rings are also readily available.


Versatile Applications:Ideal for beginners, students, and advanced artists, this airbrush kit excels in a wide range of applications. It seamlessly handles various paint spray media, from hobbies and crafts to auto graphics, models, temporary tattoos, cake decorating, fine art, makeup, nail art, and more. It's compatible with acrylic paint, body art ink, food colors, nail polish, and airbrush foundation. Just remember to dilute the solution to a milk-like consistency for optimal performance.


Comprehensive Customer Support:Included in the package are an instruction manual and an official website address card, enabling you to set up and operate the airbrush with ease. Visit our official website, where we've established a comprehensive airbrush resource center, featuring guidance on usage, disassembly, troubleshooting, and other invaluable resources. Our responsive customer service team is always ready to assist you with any challenges you may encounter, so please don't hesitate to reach out!
