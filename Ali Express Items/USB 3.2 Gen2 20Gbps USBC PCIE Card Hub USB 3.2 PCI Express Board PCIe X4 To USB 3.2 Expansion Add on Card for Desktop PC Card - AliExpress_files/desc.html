PCIe X4 To USB 3.2 Expansion Add on Card 20Gbps Adapter 3x USB3.2 and 2x TPYC-C Support Windows 7/8/10 (32/64 Bit) Mac OS 10.8.2
Features:
1. High-Speed Data Transfer: This pcie x4 to usb 3.2 expansion add on card features 5 USB 3.2 ports, including 3 Type-A and 2 Type-C ports. One USB port shares 10Gbps transfer speed, while the other four ports share another 10Gbps total transfer speed, for a combined speed of up to 20Gbps. Each port can transmit data at speeds up to 10Gbps when used separately.
2. Wide Compatibility: The adapter is compatible with Windows 7/8/10 (32/64-bit) and Mac OS 10.8.2 and above. Only one driver needs to be installed, and it can operate without drivers on Windows 10, Mac OS, and Linux.
3. Safe Use: The usb gen2 adapter is easy to install and draws power directly from the PCIe interface. The Type-A ports provide up to 5W of power, while the Type-C ports provide up to 15W. All ports have short circuit protection.
4. Cutting-Edge Chipset: This pcie x4 to usb 3.2 expansion add on card uses a cutting-edge chipset to provide you with a professional computer port expansion solution, featuring excellent circuits and secure designs.
5. Note: Older motherboards may not support bandwidth speeds of up to 20Gbps. PCIe x4 requires at least version 3.0, PCIe x8 requires at least version 2.0, and PCIe x16 requires at least version 1.0.
